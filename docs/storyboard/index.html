Karlsberg Quest

A German-language browser action RPG on the real Karlsberg near Homburg (Saar), where Duke Karl II. August’s palace stood from 1778 to 1793. You travel between today and four moments of the past, guided by Laub, a surveyor from 1840 who lives in a brass compass.

Creatures

Slow mist that lunges.

Anything hurts them: the tutorial foe.

Streuner

Stray hounds of the pack: fast, flanking, pouncing.

Dodge the pounce, hit the recovery.

Irrlichter

Floating lights with cold sparks.

Immune until the Bannlicht hits them.

Mahnbriefe · Foliant

Unpaid decrees swarm with paper cuts.

The Foliant blocks with its board.

Nebelweber · Rufer

A caster spinning frost orbs, and a caller who summons small fry.

Raffschatten · Glutlinge · Scherbling

Armoured stone eaters, and crumb swarms from the robbed-out trenches.

Items

A few key things you carry.

  • MessstabYour survey staff and weapon. Later: iron shoe, silver wire, nine notches, Laub’s plumb bob.
  • Laterne · BannlichtLantern light, and a flare that makes ghosts vulnerable.
  • Die BussoleLaub’s brass compass. It points where the land remembers.
  • Seiten der ChronikSoll’s counter-ledger: what the court never paid. The fire scattered it.
  • Das HornJost’s horn, handed over mouthpiece first.
  • Türgriff · Dukat · UhrThe one thing you save from the burning Festsaal.
  • Josts HundemarkeA tag without a name, for the dog nobody counted.
  • Solls RechenpfennigA brass counter from the Registratur’s reckoning board.
  • Matthes’ ZeichenA pebble with his mark: an open angle, three dots.
  • KasuarfederBlack, scruffy, freely given. Allegedly.
  • Kräuterbalsam · Riechsalz · LampenölHeals, restores breath, refills the lantern.
  • KalkstaubBurnt lime. Thrown, it holds ghosts for a breath.
